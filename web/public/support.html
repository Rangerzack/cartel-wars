Getting help

Email support@rangelab.io. Tell us your street name, the email on your account and what happened. For a bug, a screenshot helps.

Never send your password. We will never ask for it.

Reporting a player

  1. Open their profile and tap 🚩 Report.
  2. Pick what's wrong: their name, avatar, bio or something else.
  3. Add a note if it helps, and send it. An admin will take a look.

For something they wrote in chat, a direct message or the forum, report their profile and quote it in the note. If someone threatens you or posts personal information, email us as well.

Blocking someone

To stop hearing from a player, open their profile and tap 🚫 Block.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Account → Delete account and type your street name to confirm. Your account is gone for good: your street name, profile, cash, diamonds and items can't be brought back, and neither can your chat messages, DMs and forum posts.

If you run a crew, your Co-Capo takes over, or else the member who has played longest. A crew of one disbands. Crew and cartel banks stay with the crew and the cartel. Trades and other players' activity about you stay, without your name.

If you pay for the Daily Drop, cancel it in your device settings first. Deleting your account doesn't stop Apple from billing you.

Can't sign in? Email us from the address on your account, and we'll delete it for you.

Cancelling the Daily Drop

Right now the Daily Drop is free. To stop it, tap Cancel on the Daily Drop card on Home.

Once it's a paid subscription through Apple, cancel it on your iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cartel Wars. It stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Crates already left for you stay yours to open.

Resetting your password

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password?
  2. Enter your email and tap Send reset link.
  3. Open the link in the email. It opens in your browser. Set a new password there.
  4. Come back to the game and sign in with your new password.

Already signed in? Change it in Profile → Account → Change password.

Didn't get an email?

Check your spam or junk folder. A reset link works once and expires after a while, so if one doesn't work, ask for a new one. Still nothing after a few minutes, or a confirmation link that won't work? Email us.
