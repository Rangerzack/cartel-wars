Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: we collect what the game needs to run. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Cartel Wars is made and run by Range Lab ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Cartel Wars app and website. It says what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

What we collect and why

  • Your account. Your email address and password. We use your email to sign you in, confirm your account, send password reset links and answer you when you write to us. We don't send marketing email. Your password is stored as a one-way hash, so nobody can read it, including us.
  • A user ID. A random ID that links your account to your game.
  • Your profile. Your street name, avatar (an emoji) and bio. These are public to other players.
  • What you write. Chat messages, direct messages, and forum threads and replies. We store them so the game can show them to the people they're meant for.
  • Your game. Your cash, product, gear, diamonds, crew and cartel, fights, casino hands, territory and the times things happened. The game can't run without it.
  • Purchases, if any. In-app purchases of diamonds and a Daily Drop subscription may be offered. If you buy something, Apple takes the payment and RevenueCat, the service that processes our purchases, tells us what you bought and whether your subscription is active. We never see your card or your Apple account details.
  • Moderation records. Reports you send and reports made about you, with a snapshot of the reported profile and any note. The players you block. The actions admins take, with a log that keeps the old value, such as a name before it was reset.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records basic request details, such as IP address and time, to keep the service working and secure. We don't use them to track you.
  • On your device. The app keeps your sign-in session and a few settings, like your last bet size, in local storage on your device. There are no tracking cookies.

We don't ask for your real name, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the United States (Supabase's US West region, in California). Data travels between your device and those servers over HTTPS. If you play from outside the US, your data is stored and processed in the US.

Who sees what

  • Other players see your street name, avatar and bio, what your profile shows (like your reputation, fight record, crew and cartel), and what you post in public chat, crew and cartel chat and the forum. A direct message is seen by you and the player you send it to.
  • Admins see reports, the reported content and the moderation log. They use them to keep the game safe.
  • Range Lab can access the database to run the game, fix problems and look into reports and abuse. That can include messages when a report or a problem needs it.
  • Service providers. Supabase hosts the database and runs sign-in, including the emails it sends you. If you make a purchase, Apple and RevenueCat process it under their own privacy policies. They get only what they need for that job.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no trackers in the game, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

We'll hand over data if the law requires it, and only what is required.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your email and password, your profile and everything in your game, your chat messages, both sides of your direct message conversations, and your forum replies and threads (with the replies in them).

Some records stay so the game still adds up for other players: fights, market trades, crew and cartel bank records, the territory log and other players' activity feeds. They're anonymized: they no longer show your name or link to you. The moderation log keeps what admins did, such as a name they reset, without a link to your account. We also keep a count of deleted accounts (the date, how many days the account existed and whether it ever paid for anything) that says nothing about who you were.

Deleted data can sit in our host's backups for a short time until they expire.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account and type your street name to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away, and it can't be undone.

You can also email support@rangelab.io from the address on your account, and we'll delete it for you.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel the Daily Drop or any other subscription bought through Apple. Cancel it in your device settings.

Your data

You can change your avatar and bio in Profile at any time. To get a copy of your data, or to fix something you can't change yourself, email us.

Children

Cartel Wars is for adults. You must be 18 or older to play. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it. If you think a child is using the game, email us.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never stored in plain text. Players can't read or change the database directly; every change goes through the game's own server functions, which check who is asking. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data, we'll tell you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll also post it in the game before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or anything else: support@rangelab.io.
